Welcome to United In Grace Ministries

United In Grace Ministries is a Christ-centered nonprofit dedicated to serving our neighbors experiencing homelessness throughout Kansas City with compassion, dignity, and the love of Jesus Christ.

Every Saturday, in partnership with Hope Faith Homeless Assistance Campus, our volunteers prepare and serve hot breakfasts, fresh coffee, bottled water, hygiene supplies, prayer, encouragement, and practical resources for those in need.

We believe every person is created in the image of God and deserves to be treated with kindness, respect, and genuine compassion. More than meeting physical needs, we strive to build relationships, offer hope, and share the life-changing message of the Gospel.

✝️ Our Mission

Our mission is to demonstrate the love of Jesus Christ by serving people experiencing homelessness through compassion, practical support, meaningful relationships, and the hope of the Gospel.
